Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

DailyJournal Backup is a personal tool used only by its developer. It copies the developer's own journal backups to the developer's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot read any other file in the Drive.

What it does with data

Backup files are uploaded straight from the developer's computer to the developer's Drive. They are not sent to any other server, shared with any third party, used for advertising, or read by anyone else.

Retention and deletion

The files stay in the Drive account until the account owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the GitHub profile that hosts this page.
